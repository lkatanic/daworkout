daworkout

A free workout log for iPhone, Apple Watch and Mac.

Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. Your workouts stay on your devices and sync through your own iCloud.

My workouts aren't showing on another device.
Make sure both devices are signed in to the same iCloud account, and that daworkout is allowed to use iCloud (Settings → your name → iCloud → See All). Then open the app on each and give it a minute.

Does it need an Apple Watch?
No. With a Watch you can log sets from your wrist and record the workout so it counts toward your rings.

What does it do with my Health data?
It reads heart rate, calories, workouts and bodyweight to show next to your log. It only writes the workouts your Watch records. Nothing is sent to us. See the privacy policy.
