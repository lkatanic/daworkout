Privacy Policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

daworkout is a free workout log for iPhone, Apple Watch and Mac. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We don't collect any of your data, and we can't see it.

Where your data lives

Your workouts, templates, plans, gyms and settings are stored on your devices. If you're signed in to iCloud, they sync between your devices through your own private iCloud database. Apple stores that data under your Apple Account, and we have no access to it.

To delete it, delete the app's data in iCloud settings on your iPhone (Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage), or delete the app from every device.

Apple Health

With your permission, daworkout reads your heart rate, active calories, Apple Watch workouts and their routes, and your bodyweight and body measurements. It uses them to show next to the workouts you log and on Progress.

daworkout only writes one thing to Health: when the Apple Watch app records a workout in place of Apple's Workout app, it saves that workout with its route or place and how hard you said it was.

Health data is never sent to us or any third party, and never used for advertising.

Location

Location is used only if you give a gym a place:

  • When the app opens or a workout starts, it checks which of your gyms you're at.
  • With "Notify Me When I Arrive" on, it notices when you arrive at one of your gyms.

Your location is only compared with your gyms' places. It isn't kept or sent anywhere.

When the Apple Watch records an outdoor workout, it records the route, and for indoor workouts where they took place. These are saved to Apple Health with the workout.

Calendar

With Show in Calendar on, daworkout adds your weekly plan to a calendar named Workouts and keeps it up to date. It doesn't change any of your other calendars.

Friends

If you invite friends, daworkout shares a card with them through iCloud sharing. The card holds:

  • the name, color and figure you choose
  • weekly workout counts and streaks
  • your recent workouts and best lifts
  • your templates and schedule, if you choose to share them

Only people you invite can see it. You can stop sharing at any time, and the card is removed from iCloud. The card never passes through us.

Sharing plans

When you share a template or schedule, daworkout makes a file that you send yourself. Only the person you send it to gets it.

Summaries

Workout summaries are written by Apple Intelligence on your device. Your data doesn't leave the device to write them.

AI assistants on Mac

The Mac app can serve your log to AI assistants on that Mac. This is off unless you turn it on. When on, it's reachable only from that Mac, and only by apps you've given its access token. What an assistant does with your data is covered by that assistant's own privacy policy.

Children

daworkout isn't directed at children under 13, and it collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
